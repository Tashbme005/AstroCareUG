Emergency guide

  1. Stay calm and anchored. Restrain yourself and the patient.
  2. Call for help. Alert the crew, name a crew medical officer, open a link to the flight surgeon.
  3. Check the person. Not responding or not breathing: start CPR and use the AED.
  4. Heavy bleeding: press firmly with clean cloth and keep pressing.
  5. Chest pain, face drooping, confusion or fainting: stop, rest, use oxygen, tell ground now.
  6. Fire, smoke, toxic air or pressure loss: use mission emergency procedures first.
